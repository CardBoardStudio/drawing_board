Clean analysis-only causal contrasts suggested
- First-yard geometry only: force inward while holding its production yield at the original first-yard yield. A production hook can isolate this without changing what other hexes later produce. Compare with yield-only and full inward interventions. Ordinary inward-first changes BOTH geometry and yield.
- Yield only: retain East launch position, substitute Energy for the first yard's production, leaving build/path order fixed. Treat this as an artificial intervention rather than legal gameplay.
- Per-player path tie order: transform only movementNeighbors/nearestGoal/pathToCoord order using home isometry. Do not globally rewrite DIRECTIONS before generateGalaxy, because that changes resource/sector construction as well as paths.
- Build tie order: reorder only equal-score candidate tie breaks relative to the player's home; avoid changing scores or affordability simultaneously. Explicit inward-first is a narrower initial intervention than changing every build tie.
- Equal complete resource palette: reflect the complete home palette, not just its first yard. Then cross with path ordering; later Data-Harvester ties could mediate the outcome too.

Interpretation limit
These probes establish deterministic routes by which home orientation and stable heuristic order can alter the same bot's choices. They do not quantify how much of the observed win-share gap each route causes. Root's paired interventions are needed for that claim. The evidence does not support attributing the gap solely to planet-distance fairness or solely to turn order.
