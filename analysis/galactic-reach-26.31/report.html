Audit and reproducibility

  • Seat rotations share one setup per seed; seed blocks change neutral identities, resources and moons. World geometry remains the approved fixed layout.
  • Current26.31 and archived25.30 play separate matched games. Results compare the combined single-use order and Fleet-cap changes, not an isolated cause or a policy head-to-head.
  • No game-rule or bot-policy tuning was performed during this study. The UI correction changes CSS only; production engine hashes remain unchanged.
  • Storage expenditure is separated from construction, scoring and deck spending. Flow rates include all mixed games, including cutoffs.
  • Strategy win shares and gift effects are conditional on completed games; cutoff outcomes receive no invented winner.192long-horizon checks use the first16mixed-game seed blocks.
  • Gift effects are policy-dependent, not intrinsic exchange values. Each of8contrasts has192paired games over16seed blocks and receives family-adjusted intervals. Late gifts in games already finished remain in the intention-to-treat comparison.
