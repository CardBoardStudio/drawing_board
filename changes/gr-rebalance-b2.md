**Toned down:** Ascendancy Codex now gives 1 Prestige per use and no longer draws, Dyson Lattice 4 Prestige, Hyperspace Vaults +1 to each limit per use, and the Folded Warehouse relic is used once (+2 to each limit, draw two, then trash it). **Cheaper or stronger:** Fold-Space Gate (6 Energy, 2 Data), World Engine (6 Matter, 2 Energy, 2 Biomass), Genesis Charter (2 Matter, 7 Biomass, 1 Data), Procurement Hub (5 Data), and Colony Pods colonizes for 0 Biomass. **Replaced:** Trade Syndicate leaves the market; **Torpedo Barrage** (M11: +3 hits and prevent 3 hits, 6 Energy, 2 Biomass, 2 Data) joins the military cards.
