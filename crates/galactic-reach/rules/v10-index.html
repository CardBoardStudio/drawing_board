New in this playtest Fleets · permanent storage upgrades · direct relic recovery
  • Expand storage from your resource panel: pay 2 Matter, 1 Energy, and 1 Data for a permanent +5 capacity during your Operations or Buy turn. Repeat while you can afford it. Storage rules
  • Recover relics directly: use Construct with an Explorer and pay the former station cost. No building is placed; three recoveries unlock relic exchanges. Relic rules
  • A smaller unit roster: Settlers move 1, Fleets move 2, and Explorers move 3. Dreadnoughts, Research Stations, Gates, and Gate Synchronization are removed. Movement rules

    How to command your faction

    Your turn

    Convert cards for resources, then use one order, offer a trade, or pass. Passing immediately starts your Buy turn. Finish Buy, then the remaining factions continue Operations.

    Your round

    Produce → draw five → Operations (pass → your Buy turn) → everyone finishes → cleanup → resource directive → victory check.

    Your deck

    Normally buy one card. Spend Data to upgrade original starters or trash eligible discarded cards for 1 Data each, including starters. There is no one-card trash limit. Cards go to discard; stored resources carry forward.

    Your goal

    Reach 20 Prestige at the end of a round. Colonies, Shipyards, Relics, purchased cards, and public awards all contribute. The rulebook includes costs, scoring, and current card clarifications.
