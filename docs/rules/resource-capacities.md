# Resource capacities 28.33

The intended rules are established in the canonical rulebook at `#resources` and `#storage-allocation` before implementation. Each resource starts at capacity 4. A paid upgrade grants exactly four indivisible capacity points, allocated by the player across the four types. Costs retain the per-faction escalation: 1 of each, 2 of each, etc. Capacity points award no Prestige. Payment and allocation are atomic; cancellation pays nothing.

All resource gains retain their full amounts before excess is resolved per type. A shortage of Matter space cannot use unused Data space. Only over-limit types are legal overflow discards, with partial resolution supported. Bank gains can create overflow; player trades still require both parties to fit their own limits after settlement.

The desktop resource mat shows each stock alongside its own limit and round production. An allocation dialog shows the current and proposed limits, counts points, validates an exact allocation, and supports keyboard operation. Cradle of Lost Suns grants four free capacity points through the same allocator, followed by its existing 2 Biomass and draw-one effect; it does not advance the paid tier. Allocation and card commitment happen together, before drawing. Cancelling or reloading an uncommitted allocation draft spends nothing.

A Harvester yields one resource total, with no extra base production on its hex. The user explicitly removed Harvester upgrading: all buildings now have one level and no upgrades. Construction costs, unoccupied ordinary hex production, combat-fleet production and the 20-Prestige target remain unchanged. The user confirmed four freely allocated capacity points for Cradle of Lost Suns. Desktop readability also increases: single pieces fill most of a hex, crowded hexes use a shared grid, and player-facing labels and controls use larger type.
