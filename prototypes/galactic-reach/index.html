    QUICK REFERENCE

    How to command your faction

    Round

    Production → Draw → alternating Operations → Buy → Cleanup. The first-player marker rotates every round.

    Cards

    At the start of your turn, play any number of cards for their resource mode. Then play one order, make your one Trade action, or pass.

    Control

    Colonies produce their planet resource. Harvesters add one. Warehouses add five storage. Shipyards build ships and defend.

    Combat

    Both sides roll; 5–6 hits. Scouts roll 1, Frigates 2, Dreadnoughts 4, and Shipyards 3. Combat lasts up to three volleys.

    Conquest

    Occupy an enemy planet, then later use Colonize with a Scout and . Captured worlds receive a conquest marker.

    Victory

    Reach 15 Prestige. Finish the round, then compare Prestige, Colonies, and stored resources.
