    How to command your faction

    Read the canonical rulebook · 18.23 ↗

    Your turn

    Convert cards for resources, then use one order, offer a trade, or pass. Passing immediately starts your Buy turn. Finish Buy, then the remaining factions continue Operations.

    Your round

    Produce → draw five → Operations (pass → your Buy turn) → everyone finishes → cleanup → victory check → next round.

    Your deck

    Normally buy one card. Upgrade original starters for two of one chosen resource type each. Trash eligible discarded cards, including starters, for three of one chosen resource type each. There is no one-card trash limit. Cards go to discard; stored resources carry forward.

    Your goal

    Reach 10 Prestige at the end of a round. Colonies, Shipyards, excavated relics, purchased cards, and decisive combat victories all contribute. The rulebook includes costs, scoring, and current card clarifications.
