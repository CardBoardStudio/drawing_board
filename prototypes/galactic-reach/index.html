New in this playtest 20-point game and stronger rivals
  • 20 points to win: Colonies score 3 each, relics 2 each, decisive combat victories 1 each. No Colony pair bonus. Scoring.
  • Storage: add 5 capacity for 1 of each resource, then 2 each, 3 each, and so on. Free relic storage does not raise the price. Resources.
  • Stronger rivals: safer Settler movement, Fleet escorts and regrouping, earlier production and starter upgrades.
  • Probe before excavating: arrival reveals terrain only. Each Explorer grants Probe and Excavate to discard. Use Probe with your Explorer on a world or moon to locate its relic privately. Discovery · Excavation.
  • Bank resource buttons: select a resource on each side, then Convert four for one. Also available in existing 21.26 games. Bank exchanges.
  • Points before infrastructure: rivals claim Colonies and excavate before discretionary construction. Computer rivals.
  • Purposeful movement: Settlers seek neutral worlds or captures, Explorers hold relic sites, and fleets favor useful fights. They reinforce against visible threats. Movement · Combat.
  • Useful decks and spending: rivals preserve needed orders, buy selectively, and keep resources for scoring. They use the scoring and escalating storage costs shown above. Deck building · Scoring.
  • Public planets and randomized hidden moons remain. The Comms Feed stays below Victory points.

    How to command your faction

    Read the canonical rulebook · 23.28 ↗

    Your turn

    Convert cards for resources, then use one order, offer a trade, or pass. Passing immediately starts your Buy turn. Finish Buy, then the remaining factions continue Operations.

    Your round

    Produce → draw five → Operations (pass → your Buy turn) → everyone finishes → cleanup → victory check → next round.

    Your deck

    Normally buy one card. Upgrade original starters for two of one chosen resource type each. Trash eligible discarded cards, including starters, for three of one chosen resource type each. There is no one-card trash limit. Cards go to discard; stored resources carry forward.

    Your goal

    Reach 20 Prestige at the end of a round. Colonies, excavated relics, and decisive combat victories contribute. Click a point total to see its breakdown. The rulebook includes costs, scoring, and current card clarifications.
